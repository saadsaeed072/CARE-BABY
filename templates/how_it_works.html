Frequently Asked Questions

All babysitters on our platform undergo a thorough verification process including ID verification (CNIC), background checks, and phone verification. Verified babysitters receive a badge on their profile.

You can pay securely through our platform using multiple payment methods including credit/debit cards, EasyPaisa, and JazzCash. Payment is only processed after the babysitter confirms your booking.

You can cancel a booking through your dashboard. Cancellations made more than 24 hours before the scheduled time receive a full refund. Late cancellations may incur a fee.

Babysitter rates vary based on experience, location, and qualifications. On our platform, rates typically range from Rs. 300 to Rs. 2000 per hour. You can filter by price range when searching.
