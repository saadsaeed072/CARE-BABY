For Parents

  1. Click "Register" in the top navigation menu.
  2. Select "Parent" as your account type.
  3. Fill in your full name, email, phone number, password, and city.
  4. Click "Register" to create your account.
  5. Login with your email and password to access your dashboard.

  1. Click "Find Babysitters" in the navigation bar.
  2. Use filters to search by city, hourly rate, experience level, and language.
  3. Browse babysitter profiles to see ratings, reviews, and availability.
  4. Click on a babysitter's profile for detailed information.
  5. Only verified babysitters (with a green badge) appear in search results.

  1. Visit the babysitter's profile and click "Book Now".
  2. Select a date, start time, and end time.
  3. Enter the number of children and their ages.
  4. Add any special instructions (allergies, routines, etc.).
  5. Review the total cost and confirm the booking.
  6. The babysitter will receive a notification and can accept or decline.

To cancel a booking:

  1. Go to Dashboard → My Bookings.
  2. Find the booking you want to cancel.
  3. Click the "Cancel" button.
  4. Provide a reason for cancellation.
Note: We recommend cancelling at least 24 hours before the booking time. Frequent late cancellations may affect your account standing.

After a booking is marked as completed:

  1. Go to Dashboard → My Bookings.
  2. Find the completed booking.
  3. Click "Leave Review".
  4. Give a star rating (1-5) and write your feedback.
  5. Your review helps other parents make informed decisions!

  1. Go to Dashboard → Messages.
  2. Select an existing conversation or start a new one.
  3. Type your message and click "Send".
  4. You'll receive notifications when the babysitter replies.

For Babysitters

  1. Click "Register" and select "Babysitter" as your account type.
  2. Fill in your personal details and create a password.
  3. After registration, complete your profile with experience, skills, and photo.
  4. Submit your CNIC for verification (front and back images).
  5. Wait for admin approval — you'll be notified once verified.

A complete profile attracts more parents. Make sure to add:

  • Profile photo — A clear, professional photo.
  • About Me — Describe your experience and childcare philosophy.
  • Hourly rate — Set your rate (Rs. 300 - Rs. 2,000).
  • Languages — List languages you speak (Urdu, English, etc.).
  • Special skills — Cooking, tutoring, first aid, etc.
  • Availability — Set your weekly schedule.
  • Education & certifications — Highlight any qualifications.

  1. When a parent sends a booking request, you'll get a notification.
  2. Go to Dashboard to see pending requests.
  3. Review the booking details (date, time, number of children).
  4. Click "Accept" to confirm or "Reject" to decline.
  5. After completing the session, mark it as "Completed" to receive earnings.

  1. Go to Dashboard → Availability.
  2. Select a day of the week.
  3. Set your start time and end time for that day.
  4. Click "Add" to save the time slot.
  5. You can add multiple time slots per day.
  6. Use "Blocked Dates" to mark holidays or days off.

  1. Go to Dashboard → Verification.
  2. Enter your CNIC number.
  3. Upload clear photos of your CNIC front and CNIC back.
  4. Click "Submit" to send for review.
  5. Our team will verify your documents within 24-48 hours.
  6. You'll receive a notification once approved — then your profile goes live!
Tip: Make sure your CNIC images are clear and readable for faster verification.

Payments & Earnings

The total booking cost is calculated as:

Total Cost = Hourly Rate × Number of Hours
Platform Fee = Total Cost × 10%
Babysitter Earnings = Total Cost - Platform Fee

For example, if the hourly rate is Rs. 500 for 4 hours:
Total = Rs. 2,000 | Platform Fee = Rs. 200 | Babysitter receives = Rs. 1,800

BabyCare charges a 10% platform fee on each booking. This covers:
  • Payment processing and security
  • 24/7 customer support
  • Background verification of babysitters
  • Platform maintenance and improvements

  1. Go to Dashboard → Earnings.
  2. Check your available balance.
  3. Click "Request Withdrawal".
  4. Choose your payment method: Bank Transfer, EasyPaisa, or JazzCash.
  5. Enter the amount (minimum Rs. 1,000).
  6. Submit and wait for admin approval (typically 1-3 business days).

Safety & Trust

Our verification process includes:

  • CNIC Verification — Front and back of national ID are verified.
  • Background Check — Our team reviews each applicant's background.
  • Admin Approval — Only admin-approved profiles go live.
  • Ongoing Monitoring — Reviews and ratings are monitored continuously.

Yes! BabyCare takes data security seriously:

  • All passwords are hashed and encrypted.
  • Personal details are only shared with confirmed booking parties.
  • CNIC images are securely stored and only accessible by admins.
  • We never sell or share your data with third parties.

If you have any safety concerns, please:

  • Visit our Contact Page and select "Babysitter Complaint" as the subject.
  • Call our support line at 0300-1234567.
  • WhatsApp us for quick response.
Emergency: For immediate safety concerns, contact local authorities first, then notify us.
